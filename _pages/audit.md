## Free 45-minute Operations Fit Call

This is the right conversation once you want a human read on fit. Not sure it’s worth 45 minutes yet? Take the [Margin Leak Check](/leak-check/) first — eight questions, about three minutes, no calendar.

The Fit Call diagnoses three things:

1. **The pressure** — what decision you’re actually trying to make, and where cash, margin, or operating control is at risk
2. **What’s knowable** — what evidence you already have, what’s missing, and whether waste can be located cleanly
3. **Feasibility** — what it would realistically take to settle the question and what ROI looks like

You’ll leave with an honest recommendation: a first scoped diagnostic, a durable workflow, scoped ongoing support, or **no fit**. No commitment, no follow-up sales sequence.

---

## Situations that usually fit

- Margins slipping with no shared explanation
- Growth outrunning day-to-day control
- Cash timing or collections that don’t reconcile cleanly with activity
- Leadership arguing about the numbers that drive action
- Turnaround, ownership change, or cost pressure that needs clarity fast

If the ROI path isn’t believable, I’ll tell you that too. That filter saves both of us time.
