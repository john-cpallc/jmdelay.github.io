Changepoint Analytics is an operations intelligence practice. I help owners and financial leaders connect messy operational data to cash, margin, and capacity decisions — then build the smallest reliable system needed to act.

I'm John DeLay. I take on a limited number of clients at a time, scope work to specific outcomes, and hand off the logic so your team can run it. Based in Birmingham, AL; most work is remote. Healthcare and regulated environments are natural depth from my background — the practice is open to strong problems elsewhere.

How engagements work is on the [Services](/services/) page. Ready to talk? [Book a Fit Call](/audit/).

## Why clients trust the work

**Education:** MSPH in Biostatistics, University of Alabama at Birmingham — BS in Mathematics, Birmingham-Southern College.

**Background:** Health tech (AI/LLM systems, prior authorization), regional banking (risk model validation, SR 11-7), Medicare quality improvement (CMS programs, claims analytics, EHR data), published public health research.

That depth stays under the hood. On the surface, the work is operational ROI — clearer cash, margin, and capacity decisions.

## Practice design

Intentionally lean. Fixed-scope work, client-owned output, honest no-fit when the ROI path isn’t there. The point is durable leverage for operators — not a large firm and not open-ended advisory disguised as a retainer.
